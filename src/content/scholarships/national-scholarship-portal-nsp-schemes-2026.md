## 🎓 National Scholarship Portal (NSP) Enrollment 2026
The **National Scholarship Portal (NSP)** registration process for central, state, and UGC/AICTE sponsored financial aid schemes is open for pre-matric, post-matric, and higher technical program students. Eligible students can apply today to receive funds directly in their Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

---

## 📋 Popular Scholarship Schemes Open on NSP
Aspirants can choose specific streams depending on eligibility:

| Scheme Name | Sponsoring Ministry | General Eligibility Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Post-Matric Scheme for Minorities** | Ministry of Minority Affairs | Class 11, 12, UG, or PG students; Min 50% marks in previous exam; Family Income < ₹2 Lakh |
| **Prime Minister Yasasvi Scheme** | Ministry of Social Justice | OBC, EBC, or DNT students studying in designated schools |
| **National Means Cum Merit (NMMS)** | Department of School Education | Class 9 students; Parents annual income < ₹3.5 Lakh |
| **Pragati Scholarship Scheme** | AICTE / Central Government | Meritorious female technology students pursuing diploma/degrees |

---

## 📁 Critical Documents Required
Applicants must upload clear scanned copies of these certificates to clear administrative inspections:
1. Student **Bonafide Certificate** from their active Institute.
2. Self-attested copy of previous qualifying examination marksheets.
3. Family Income Proof Certificate issued by competent circle officer/tehsildar.
4. Active Caste Certificate (if registering under benefits).
5. Student’s personal bank passbook showing active **Aadhaar Seeding** status.

---

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Registration & Login Guide
Submit your registration cleanly without any intermediate rejection risks:
1. Access the unified national portal: [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in).
2. Click on the **"New Registration"** tab on the home banner.
3. Provide academic details, state of domicile, and Aadhaar card verification.
4. Input your active Mobile Number and obtain the One-Time-Password (OTP).
5. Once your Application ID is created, change your login password under candidate workspace.
6. Select appropriate scholarship schemes based on criteria (e.g. Pre-matric, Post-matric, or Professional Scheme).
7. Upload relevant documents (not required for scholarship amounts below ₹50,000, except Bonafide card).
8. Verify all entries and submit. Get the physical printout signed by your head of school/college for offline physical confirmation.
