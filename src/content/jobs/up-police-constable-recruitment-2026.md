## 🎓 Academic Eligibility & Age Limit
### A. Educational Qualification
* Candidates must have successfully passed **Class 10+2 (Intermediate)** or equivalent secondary education from a recognized education board/institution in India.

### B. Age Limit (As of July 1, 2026)
* **Male Applicants:** 18 Years to 22 Years (Relaxed up to 28 for reserved categories).
* **Female Applicants:** 18 Years to 25 Years (Relaxed up to 30 for reserved categories).

## 🏃 Physical Standards Requirements (PST / PET)
To qualify for final selection, candidates must strictly clear physical criteria:

### Height and Chest Standards
* **Male (General/OBC/SC):** Height: 168 cm | Chest: 79-84 cm (Minimum 5 cm expansion required)
* **Male (ST):** Height: 160 cm | Chest: 77-82 cm
* **Female (General/OBC/SC):** Height: 152 cm | Weight: Minimum 40 kg
* **Female (ST):** Height: 147 cm | Weight: Minimum 40 kg

### Physical Endurance Run
* **Male Candidates:** 4.8 km run in under 25 minutes.
* **Female Candidates:** 2.4 km run in under 14 minutes.

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Online Application Process
Follow these precise rules to register and apply without errors:
1. Navigate directly to the official UPPRPB website: [uppbpb.gov.in](https://uppbpb.gov.in).
2. Look for the **Constable Recruitment 2026 Application Link** on the homepage.
3. Click "New Candidate Registration" and complete validation using an active mobile number and email.
4. Log back in using your generated Registration Number and Password credentials.
5. Fill up the application form including personal and academic records.
6. Upload scanned, passport-size photographs and signatures in JPEG format (sizes within 20KB-50KB).
7. Submit the ₹400 application processing fee using secure payment gateways.
8. Review your completed declaration carefully and click "Final Submit". Print a physical copy for verification.
