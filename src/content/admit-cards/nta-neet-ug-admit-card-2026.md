## 🎟️ NTA NEET UG Admit Card Released
The National Testing Agency (NTA) has officially published the Hall Tickets and Admit Cards for undergraduate engineering/medical aspirants scheduled for the **National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET-UG)**. Eligible candidates are advised to download and verify exam details properly using their login applications.

---

## 🗓️ Crucial Exam Milestones
| Milestone | Timeline/Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Exam Date** | 2026-06-28 (Sunday) |
| **Mode of Examination** | Offline (Pen and Paper-based OMR Test) |
| **Duration of Exam** | 3 Hours 20 Minutes (02:00 PM to 05:20 PM) |
| **Admit Card Release Date** | 2026-06-10 |
| **Official Website** | [exams.nta.ac.in/NEET](https://exams.nta.ac.in/NEET) |

## 🎒 Mandatory Documents to Carry on Exam Day
Candidates must report to their allotted center with the following essentials, failing which entry will be strictly denied:
1. **Printed Copy of NEET-UG Admit Card:** Colored print on A4 sheet with postcard size photograph pasted on the specified blank frame.
2. **Passport Size Photo:** Same as uploaded on the online application form to be pasted on the attendance sheet.
3. **Valid ID Proof (Original):** Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, driving license, or Passport.
4. **Authorized Postcard-Size Photograph:** Pasted securely on the downloaded Proforma page.

---

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Directions to Download Admit Card
Aspirants can pull their official hall tickets using these instructions:
1. Navigate to the unified results portal of NTA: [exams.nta.ac.in/NEET](https://exams.nta.ac.in/NEET).
2. Click the highlighted ticker: **"Download Admit Card for NEET UG 2026 Exam"**.
3. Under the input fields, enter your unique **Application Number**.
4. Input your **Date of Birth (DOB)** in DD/MM/YYYY format.
5. Provide the visual security PIN carefully.
6. Click **"Submit"** to load the scorecard dynamically on the screen.
7. Print out at least two colored hard copies of the three-page PDF. Keep them safe till the counselling processes conclude.
