## 📢 SSC GD Constable CBT Result Out
The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has released the results and qualifying cut-off scores for the **Constable (General Duty)** in Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs), SSF, and Rifleman (GD) in Assam Rifles Examination. ## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Procedure to Check SSC GD Results
Candidates can follow these secure instructions to look up their qualifications instantly:
1. Open the Staff Selection Commission's official website: [ssc.gov.in](https://ssc.gov.in).
2. On the top navigation menu, click on the **"Results"** tab.
3. Switch files by clicking the **"CONSTABLE-GD"** category header.
4. Locate the active link: *\"Constable (GD) in CAPFs, SSF, and Rifleman (GD) Exam 2026 - Shortlisted Candidates for Physical Endurance Test (Write-Up & List-1/List-2)\"*.
5. Click **"Result"** (PDF link) to open the selection roster immediately.
6. Use **CTRL + F** (or search in PDF reader if on mobile) and type in your **Roll Number** or **Full Name**.
7. If your name appears, save of copy of your selection rank! You are qualified to attend the PET/PST physicals.
8. To view your individual scorecards, log in via the SSC registration desk using your ID and Password before the clearance deadline.
