## 🎓 University of Delhi UG Admissions 2026 Launch
The University of Delhi (DU) has formally rolled out its **Common Seat Allocation System (CSAS)** registration dashboard for aspirants seeking seats in top colleges (Hindu, Miranada House, SRCC, St. Stephens, etc.) for the academic term 2026-2027. Admissions are processed strictly based on cumulative **CUET UG** merit scores.

---

## 🗓️ CSAS Phase-Wise Schedule
The admission calendar is divided across distinct portal stages:

| Phase | Phase Particulars | Duration/Deadlines |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Phase 1** | Application CSAS Registration & Profile Setups | 2026-06-05 to 2026-07-15 |
| **Phase 2** | Program Preferences Selection & Mid-Entry Forms | July 2026 |
| **Phase 3** | Seat Allocation Roster releases & Admission Confirmation | August 2026 |

---

## 💳 Registration Fee Breakdown
* **UR / OBC-NCL / EWS categories:** ₹250/- (Non-Refundable)
* **SC / ST / PwBD categories:** ₹100/- (Non-Refundable)
* **Sports / ECA quota trials registration:** Extra ₹100/- per selection.

---

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step CSAS Online Application Guide
Aspirants must keep their documents ready and register using the following steps:
1. Navigate directly to the official Delhi University CSAS registration gateway: [admission.uod.ac.in](https://admission.uod.ac.in).
2. Enter your unique **CUET (UG) 2026 Application Number** and verification code.
3. Your basic details (Name, Photo, Signature, and Subject choices) will automatically populate from NTA databases.
4. Input your valid class XII marksheet values and academic profile board details.
5. Upload self-attested documents: Class X Certificate, Class XII Marksheet, and Category certification (EWS/OBC-NCL/SC/ST) if applicable.
6. Complete payment of the non-refundable registration fee.
7. Post Phase-1, login again to fill in your college and course preference combinations list carefully. Lock your preferences before the priority window closes.
