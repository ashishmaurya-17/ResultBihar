## 🌾 PM Kisan 18th Installment Released
The Government of India is releasing the next installment (18th block) under the **Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)** welfare program. Millions of eligible land-holding farmer families will receive flat-rate direct benefit transfers of **₹2,000/-** sent directly to their verified bank accounts.

---

## 📋 Yojana Brief
| Parameter | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Name of Scheme** | Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) |
| **Sponsoring Body** | Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare |
| **Yearly Financial Benefit** | ₹6,000/- (Delivered in three periodic installments of ₹2,000/- each) |
| **Current Target Phase** | 18th Installment Cycle |
| **Release Date** | Quarter 2, 2026 |
| **Official Website Portal** | [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in) |

---

## 🔒 Mandate Check: Mandatory eKYC
To receive the cash benefits uninterrupted, farmers are directed to complete their **e-KYC verification** immediately:
* **Option A (Biometric/CSC):** Visit any nearby Common Service Center (CSC) to upload fingerprint verification records.
* **Option B (OTP based):** Log in online using your active Aadhaar linked mobile number to update instantly for free.

---

## 📋 Directions to Verify Status & Beneficiary Lists
Farmers can follow these easy, validated guidelines to check their status securely:
1. Open up the official PM-KISAN portal directly: [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in).
2. Look at the section **"Farmers Corner"** listed on the dashboard page.
3. Click on the action button **"Know Your Status"**.
4. Enter your unique **Registration Number** (if forgotten, use your Mobile/Aadhaar number to search).
5. Input the secure visual captchas on screen and click **"Get Data"**.
6. The window will display your payment status chart. Check if *"FTO Resolved (Installment Sent)"* appears.
7. To view villagers lists, tap **"Beneficiary List"** and select State, District, Sub-District, Block, and village from dropdown panels.
