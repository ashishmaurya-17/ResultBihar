## 📚 Official SSC CGL Syllabus Guide
To qualify for premier administrative postings like Assistant Section Officer (ASO), Income Tax Inspector, Central Excise Assistant, or Sub-Inspector, candidates must crack the **Combined Graduate Level (CGL)** examination. Below is the comprehensive, official topic layout for Tier 1 and Tier 2 stages.

---

## 📊 SSC CGL Tier-1 Examination Pattern
The Tier-1 examination acts as an qualifying preliminary filter, featuring **100 objective-type multiple-choice questions** worth a total of **200 marks**:

| Section | Questions | Max Marks | Session Duration |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **General Intelligence & Reasoning** | 25 | 50 | 60 Minutes (Combined) |
| **General Awareness** | 25 | 50 | 60 Minutes (Combined) |
| **Quantitative Aptitude** | 25 | 50 | 60 Minutes (Combined) |
| **English Comprehension** | 25 | 50 | 60 Minutes (Combined) |
| **Total Marks** | **100** | **200** | **1 Hour** |

- *Negative marking:* **0.50** marks deducted for each incorrect answer.

---

## 🔍 Sectional Detailed Syllabus Topics

### 1. General Intelligence & Reasoning
* Analogy (Semantic, Symbolic, Figural)
* Classification and Venn Diagrams
* Coding-Decoding patterns
* Space visualization and Orientation
* Critical thinking and Problem Solving
* Syllogistic reasoning questions

### 2. General Awareness
* Ancient, Medieval, and Modern Indian History
* Indian Constitution, Civics, and National Polity
* Physical Geography and Agricultural profiles
* Basic Physics, Chemistry, Biology standards
* Crucial current events, sports records, and major scheme announcements

### 3. Quantitative Aptitude
* Number Systems (HCF/LCM, Decimals, Fractions)
* Percentage, Ratio & Proportion, Average
* Profit & Loss, Discount, Simple & Compound Interest
* Basic Algebra, School Algebra, Graphs of Linear Equations
* Triangle and Congruency, Circle and Tangents rules
* Height & Distance, Trigonometric ratios

### 4. English Comprehension
* Active/Passive voice conversions
* Spotting grammar errors
* Synonym & Antonym pairs
* Idioms and Phrases
* One-word substitution
* Reading Comprehension passages
